🚀 How It Works

Shopify merchants can sell directly inside Google AI Mode (Search) and the Gemini app. Customers discover products through natural conversation, get AI-curated recommendations, and complete checkout — all without leaving the chat.

This is powered by the Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP), an open standard co-developed by Shopify and Google.

  • 🔒 Works with any payment processor — Shopify Payments NOT required

⚙ UCP Technical Architecture

  • 🔗 REST (HTTPS+JSON) — primary transport binding
  • 🤖 MCP (Model Context Protocol) — LLM agents invoke UCP tools directly
  • 🔁 A2A (Agent-to-Agent) — direct agent communication
  • 📄 Capability negotiation via /.well-known/ucp

🛍 For Shopify Merchants

No setup required. Shopify merchants are UCP-enabled by default. Manage from Shopify Admin > Settings > Sales Channels > Agentic Storefronts. Toggle Google AI Mode / Gemini on or off independently of other channels (ChatGPT, Copilot). Product data syncs automatically via the Google & YouTube sales channel to Google Merchant Center.
