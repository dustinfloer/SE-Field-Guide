Source-backed behavior

        
This simulation treats Ventrata as ticketing source of truth and Shopify as storefront, cart, checkout, payment, customer, and commercial order layer. Middleware owns translation, caching, hold creation, checkout handoff state, and later order reconciliation.

        

          
Ventrata ProductsUsed for product, option, and unit mapping. Endpoint label shown as GET /products.

          
Ventrata AvailabilityCalendar browsing uses POST /availability/calendar. Exact date/time validation uses POST /availability.

          
Ventrata BookingsPre-checkout hold uses POST /bookings and displays ON_HOLD as a reservation state.

          
Shopify Storefront Cart APICheckout handoff is modeled with cartCreate and checkoutUrl, with ticket references carried as cart line attributes.

        

      

      

        
Explicit non-assumptions

        
The PDP does not call Ventrata directly. Shopify inventory is not treated as attraction capacity. Cached availability is not final truth. Post-payment confirmation, ticket delivery, cancellation, and refund reconciliation are called out as required follow-up flows, not silently assumed.

        

          
PricingSample prices are illustrative. Final pricing ownership must be verified: Shopify-managed, pre-cached, Ventrata-returned, or exact-price cart pattern.

          
Hold durationThe hold expiry is displayed as an example value. Real duration and extension support must be verified with Ventrata and implementation partner.

          
After paymentA production integration should confirm the Ventrata booking after Shopify payment and write supplier references back to the Shopify order.
